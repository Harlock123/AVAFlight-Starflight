# AVAFlight player guide

AVAFlight is an open-ended space exploration game. You command an Interstel survey ship from Starport on Arth. Your job is to:

- explore 270 star systems
- mine planets and catalogue alien life
- talk with alien races
- uncover why the stars are flaring before Arth's own sun does

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard | Gamepad |
|---|---|---|
| Move cursor / fly / drive / steer | Arrow keys, WASD, numeric keypad | D-pad or left stick (analog in flight and on the star map) |
| Select / confirm | Enter or Space | A (South) |
| Back / close / menu | Esc or Backspace | B (East) |
| Fire (combat) | Space, Enter or F | X (West), A |
| Star map | M | Back / Select |
| Crew status / Ship status / Cargo / Log | C / I / G / L | — |
| Hail (in an encounter) | H | — |
| Scan (in orbit) | N | Y (North) |
| Zoom | + / − | — |
| Pause (Modern) | P | Start |
| Quick save | F5 | — |
| Fullscreen | F11 or Alt+Enter | — |

- **Remapping.** Every keyboard binding can be changed in **Settings → Key bindings**. Press Tab to switch to the bindings list, then Enter on an action to rebind it.
- **Mouse.** Every menu item can be clicked, and lists scroll with the wheel.

## Helm vs menu

In flight, on a planet, and in combat, the arrow keys **steer** (the "HELM" indicator is shown at top right). Press **Esc** to give the arrows to the **control panel** menu instead, and Enter on "Helm" to go back.

## The console

| Area | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Main view (left) | Space, the planet, the terrain or combat |
| Auxiliary view (top right) | Fuel, hull, armour, shields, weapons, cargo and crew health |
| Control panel (right) | Crew posts, then that post's functions |
| Text window (bottom) | Messages |

## Starport

| Module | What you do there |
|---|---|
| **Operations** | **Read the notices first.** They contain leads and warnings, and Endurium price changes. Also the mission briefing, and colony recommendations for planets you have logged. |
| **Personnel** | Create crew. Five races, each with different starting and maximum skills, durability (resistance to injury) and learning rate. Train skills at 300 M.U. per session. Deleting a crew member does not refund training. |
| **Crew Assignment** | Fill all six posts. One person may hold several posts. |
| **Bank** | Balance and the last 10 transactions. Interest is 12% per year, paid when you dock. |
| **Ship Configuration** | Engines, shields, armour, missiles and lasers (classes 1–5); cargo pods (50 m³, 500 M.U., up to 16); repairs; naming the ship. Mass slows acceleration; armour is heavy. |
| **Trade Depot** | Sell minerals, specimens and analysed artifacts. Buy or sell Endurium. Analyse artifacts (500 M.U.). |
| **Docking Bay** | Launch, once the pre-flight checklist is clear. |

You start with **12,000 M.U.**, a class 1 engine and 20 m³ of Endurium. A sensible start: four cargo pods, a crew, and a little training.

## Crew posts

| Post | Skill | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Captain | — | Commands. Land, launch, dock, cargo, log planets, save. |
| Science Officer | Science | How much the sensors and analysis reveal. Above 150, aliens are identified at long range. Also the vehicle's mineral scanner range. |
| Navigator | Navigation | Weapon accuracy; seeing fluxes (above 150); re-fixing position after a flux; keeping the vehicle on course in storms (200+). |
| Engineer | Engineering | Speed of in-flight repairs. Heavy damage uses repair minerals: cobalt, molybdenum, aluminum, titanium, promethium. |
| Communications | Communication | How much alien speech is translated. +25 if any crew member is of the alien's race, +50 if the officer is. |
| Doctor | Medicine | Healing speed and treatment. |

**Crew death is permanent.** When someone dies, the most capable surviving crew member covers the post. If everyone dies, the game is over.

## Navigation

- **Hyperspace.** Fly with the arrows. **M** opens the star map: move the cursor and press **Enter** to lay in a straight cruise course. The readout shows distance, fuel and travel time.
- **Fuel.** Endurium is both fuel and money. Hyperspace costs 0.48 m³ per coordinate with a class 1 engine, falling to 0.16 with class 5. Shields use 0.1 m³ per hour. Landing and launch each cost 0.25 m³ per G.
- **Out of fuel.** Use **Communications → Distress call** to be towed home for a hefty fee.
- **Star systems.** Fly over a star to enter it. Inside, fly over a planet and choose **Navigator → Orbit planet**. Fly past the system's edge to return to hyperspace. **Don't fly into the star.**
- **Stellar condition.** Reported when you enter a system. Being in a system on the day its star flares is fatal.
- **Continuum fluxes** jump you across the map. A navigator with skill over 150, or a certain artifact, can see them. Others may hit them by accident and lose their position for a while.
- **Nebulae** disable shields.

## Planets

In orbit:

- **Science → Sensors / Analysis** surveys the planet. Uncertain readings show "NOT CERTAIN".
- **Captain → Log planet** enters an analysed planet in the log so it can be recommended as a colony.
- **Captain → Land** opens the landing map. Known ruins, artifacts and messages are marked. Never land on worlds over 8 G.

On the surface, the **terrain vehicle**:

- holds 50 m³
- burns fuel per step, more in mountains and in extreme temperatures
- if it runs dry, the crew walks back to the ship and the vehicle is lost (replacement charged at Starport)

What you can find:

- **Minerals** (yellow diamonds; Endurium is magenta) are denser at altitude. Stop on a deposit and choose **Pick up**.
- **Lifeforms:** green are passive, red are hostile. **Stunner**, then **Capture specimen**: flying creatures can't be captured, and duplicate species are worth nothing. **Scan lifeforms** records bio-data instead.
- **Ruins and messages:** stop on the marker and **Pick up**. Messages are kept in the cargo list.
- **Storms** injure crew.

Drive back to the ship (white triangle) and choose **Return to ship and launch**.

## Aliens

You will meet:

- Elowan, Thrynn, Velox, Spemin, Mechan 9, Gazurtoid and Uhlek
- the Minstrels
- Nomad probes
- a strange ship that speaks only in binary

How to talk to them:

- **Hail** (H, or **Communications → Hail**) in a posture: Friendly, Hostile or Obsequious.
- Then make **statements** (which change their attitude more) or **ask** about themselves, other races, the Old Empire, the Ancients, or general information.
- Races differ. Experiment, and ask several ships the same question.
- Raised shields and armed weapons look hostile to them. Firing on peaceful ships has lasting consequences.

## Combat

Combat runs in **real time** and **cannot be paused**.

- **Navigator → Raise shields / Arm weapons.** Arming takes a few seconds.
- **Helm:** Left/Right turn, Up thrusts, Space fires along the nose. The computer uses lasers at short range and missiles at long range.
- **Escape** by flying off-screen.
- Damage hits shields first, then armour, then the hull, and can damage systems and crew.
- **Captain → Collect debris** after a battle gives salvage to sell.

## Economy

| Source | Value |
|---|---|
| Minerals | Lead 40 up to Rodnium 440 M.U./m³ |
| Endurium | 1,000 M.U./m³, rising later |
| Specimens and bio-data | Larger, more remote and predatory species are worth more |
| Analysed artifacts | Fixed values |
| Accepted colony recommendations | Tens of thousands of M.U. |
| Salvage | Varies |

Costs are equipment, training, fuel, repairs, analysis and towing. Early on, money is tight, as it was in the original: mining is how you pay for your first upgrades.

## Classic and Modern

**Classic** keeps the original's information and interface habits. **Modern** keeps every rule and number the same, and adds:

- an automatic captain's log
- star-map waypoints with notes
- a fuel-range ring and warnings
- tooltips
- a pause hotkey
- dismissible hints
- named save slots

## The story (spoilers)

- Arth's sun will flare at the end of the tenth month of 4620. Other suns have been flaring, spreading outward from the core.
- Alien races, the Old Empire's ruins and old logs point to an Ancient construct: a **Crystal Planet** (the Velox call it the "Great Egg"). It ignites life-bearing suns.
- The Ancients turn out to be the Endurium itself.
- To destroy the Crystal Planet you need:
  - the **Crystal Orb**, to survive the approach
  - the **Crystal Cone**, to find the Nexus of Control
  - a Phlegmak **Black Egg** planet-buster, planted at the Nexus before you leave.
- The Uhlek guard the rimward regions. Their single mind lives on one world, and some say destroying it would scatter them.
- Talk to everyone. Not everyone tells the truth.
